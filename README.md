# IUT Datathon — Onsite Round

Welcome to the official resource repository for the teams selected for the onsite round of the IUT Datathon.

This repository contains the materials and information shared with the selected teams for the final phase of the competition.

## Competition

- **Competition:** [Bengali Hallucination Detection](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/bengali-hallucination/overview)
- **Platform:** Kaggle

Please read the competition description and rules carefully before participating. Teams are expected to follow all competition guidelines and submit their solutions through the Kaggle competition page.

## Onsite Round Resources

The files in this repository are intended to help selected teams prepare for and participate in the onsite round. Check back regularly for updates, announcements, datasets, instructions, and other relevant materials.

## Important Notes

- Keep your team information up to date.
- Follow the competition rules and submission guidelines.
- Do not share private competition materials outside your team.
- Monitor this repository and the Kaggle competition page for announcements.

Good luck to all the selected teams!
